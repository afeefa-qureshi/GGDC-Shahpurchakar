About Our campus

Learn about our institution, mission, academic environment, and campus.

College Overview

Government Girls Degree College, Shahpur Chakar is a college built for young women. We provide good education to help our students succeed in their studies. Our classes focus on clear teaching so that every student can understand her subjects deeply. Our campus has comfortable classrooms, science labs, and a quiet library space. We provide a peaceful and secure environment where students can study comfortably, build skills, and prepare for university.

We believe in keeping our students healthy and active. Our college has open grounds where students can play sports like badminton, volleyball, and other games. We also arrange annual sports days and fun competitions to help students build teamwork, confidence, and friendly relationships.

Our Core Mission

Our goal is to give every girl a strong start in her education. We teach our students to understand their subjects well and face future challenges with confidence. To provide good education to young women in our area. We want to help our students think clearly, build useful skills, and become responsible people who help their families and community

Our Strategic Vision

We want to build a collegewhere parents feel proud to send their daughters. We aim to keep improving our classrooms and labs to give our community the best possible learning experience. We want to be known for great teaching, safe learning spaces, and helping every student build a successful future

Message from the Principal

Professor Jamila Gul Wassan.

Education is not just about copying down notes or memorizing books. It is about learning how to think, grow, and solve real-world problems. Here at our college, we encourage students to ask questions, learn through practical work in our labs, and discover their true talents. Our main goal is to help every student understand her subjects deeply and build real confidence. We do not want you to just pass exams; we want you to learn useful skills for life. Our teachers are always here to guide you and help you learn

We welcome you to a community that values hard work, supports your goals, and is fully dedicated to helping you build a successful future career."
